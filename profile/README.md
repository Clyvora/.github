### Small tools. Clear purpose.

We make browser tools for data, media, QR codes, and live train locations.

[Explore the apps](https://clyvora.tech/) · [Repositories](https://github.com/orgs/Clyvora/repositories)

## Our apps

| Project | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| [Lens](https://www.lens.clyvora.tech/) | Open, inspect, search, and filter JSON and CSV files, then export the data you need. |
| [mewvi](https://mewvi.xyz/) | Download supported public media and convert your own audio and video. Currently in beta. |
| [Live op Weg](https://liveopweg.nl/) | Follow live train locations on a map. |
| [Clyvora QR Studio](https://qr.clyvora.tech/) | Create and style QR codes for links, Wi-Fi, contacts, and more. Read codes from images and export PNG, SVG, or PDF. |

mewvi was previously called Clyvora Convert. Its source repository still uses the [Convert](https://github.com/Clyvora/Convert) name.

## Your data

- **Lens:** inspect and convert selected files on your device.
- **mewvi:** convert local audio and video on your device. Public-link downloads use Clyvora infrastructure and contact the source platform; availability depends on the provider.
- **QR Studio:** create, style, read, and export codes on your device. QR content stays in your browser.
- **Live op Weg:** use online railway data to display train locations.

Each project's documentation explains its features and data handling in more detail.

## Build with us

We welcome bug reports, accessibility fixes, and focused improvements. Open an issue in the relevant repository and include enough detail to reproduce the problem.

Before contributing, check that repository's contribution guidance and license. Licensing varies by project. Follow its security policy when reporting a vulnerability.
